# CyberBridge Summer School — Relevance to My Background

*Civil engineer (water/wastewater specialization) considering a pivot into cybersecurity*

## The Clearest Connection: OT Security

**Wed, Aug 5 — Reverse Engineering & Binary Exploitation in OT**
This is the standout session. OT (Operational Technology) refers to the industrial
control systems (SCADA, PLCs) that run physical infrastructure — and water/wastewater
treatment plants are a textbook OT environment. This is the same domain as my hydraulic
modeling work (MIKE+, AutoCAD Civil 3D), just approached from the security side.

- Attacks on water utilities via their control systems are a real and growing concern
  internationally.
- Understanding both the civil engineering side *and* the OT security side is a
  genuinely rare combination — a potential differentiator, not just "extra cybersecurity
  knowledge."

**Tue, Aug 4 — CIS18 Controls + Technical Operational Security**
Reinforces the OT angle — CIS18 and "operational security" are standard framing in
critical-infrastructure protection.
